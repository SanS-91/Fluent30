Step 2 · Workplace Signals

Recognize ownership and soft requests.

15 min
Assigning work
Could you take that?
Usually a polite task assignment, not a general question.
Volunteering
I can pick that up.
The speaker takes ownership of the task.
Deferring
Let's circle back on that.
The issue is postponed, not resolved.
Clarifying
Just to clarify, do you mean…?
Signals that the speaker is checking scope or meaning.
Deadline commitment
I can get that to you by four.
A concrete delivery commitment.
Soft uncertainty
I should be able to get that done.
Likely commitment, but slightly less certain than “I will”.

Let's circle back. Not decided yet When / with whom it will be revisited

Transcript — Maya, Chris & Leo

Maya: Okay, before we wrap, can we quickly go over the client deck?

Chris: Yeah. Most of it's done. I still need to update the revenue slide with the latest numbers.

Leo: Sorry to jump in — are we using the forecast from Monday or the revised file from this morning?

Chris: The revised one from this morning. Good catch.

Maya: Great. Chris, could you take the revenue slide and update the appendix as well?

Chris: Yep, I can pick that up. I should be able to get both done by three-thirty.

Leo: There's still one issue on the market slide. We don't have the latest competitor benchmark.

Maya: Right. Leo, can you check whether Research has sent anything new?

Leo: Sure. If they haven't, I'll use the last approved benchmark and flag it as pending.

Chris: Just to clarify, do you want the old benchmark in the deck, or just in the backup?

Maya: In the deck, but clearly marked as pending. We can circle back if Research sends an update before four.

Leo: Got it. I'll check with them now.

Maya: Perfect. If I have both updates by four, I can do a final review and send the revised deck to the client by four-thirty.

Chris: Works for me.

Leo: Same here.

“Sorry to jump in…” Polite interruption. The speaker is stopping the flow because a clarification matters now.
“Good catch.” A quick acknowledgment that the other person noticed something important.
“Could you take…?” Soft request, but functionally an assignment.
“I can pick that up.” Explicit ownership. The task now belongs to that speaker.
“flag it as pending” Keep the item visible, but clearly mark that it is not final.
“circle back” Revisit later. It does not mean the topic is solved.
