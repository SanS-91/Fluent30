How the 30 days progress

Difficulty increases gradually from decoding natural speech to handling C1-speed discussions and extended responses.

Days 01–10

Decode natural speech

Connected speech, weak forms, linking, reductions, everyday conversation and listening accuracy.

Days 11–20

Work & real-world listening

Meetings, presentations, negotiation, podcasts, interviews and adaptation to different accents.

Days 21–30

C1 processing & response

Abstract topics, complex arguments, fast listening, debate, oral summaries and extended conversation.

Daily learning loop

Every lesson follows the same progression from listening input to spoken output.

🎧
First Listen Catch the main idea without transcript.
🎯
Deep Listen Identify detail, intent and key arguments.
✍️
Dictation Decode exactly what was said.
🔍
Decode Study reductions, linking and useful chunks.
🗣️
Shadowing Copy rhythm, stress and natural delivery.
💬
Speak Back Turn listening input into spontaneous speech.

Primary listening style

Natural US English is the core model. From the second half of the course, additional international accents can be introduced to strengthen listening flexibility.

Progress is stored locally
