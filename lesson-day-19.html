Transcript — Global project update

Rachel: Morning, everyone. Quick check before we finish — are we still comfortable with Wednesday as the launch date?

Marcus: From London, yes. The client team has signed off the content. I just need to make one change to the pricing slide, and I can send that through by half three today.

Jia: Singapore is nearly there as well. We still have one payment-flow test outstanding. If the vendor can support us tomorrow morning, we should be able to close it by lunchtime.

Rachel: Could we bring that test forward to first thing tomorrow?

Jia: Can. I'll check with them after this call and revert by end of day.

Ben: On the Australia side, training is done. No major issues. I'll send the attendance and support notes through this afternoon.

Marcus: Just one thing — if the Singapore test slips, are we holding the whole launch?

Rachel: No. Wednesday stays unless the payment flow fails completely. If it's only a timing issue, Singapore can go live a little later.

Jia: Understood. So our priority is to finish the test Tuesday and flag any issue straight away.

Rachel: Exactly. I'll send the final readiness note Tuesday evening once I have all three regional updates.

“half three” Common British expression meaning 3:30, not 2:30.
“send that through” Send the document or information to someone.
“bring that test forward” Move the test to an earlier time.
“Can.” In Singaporean workplace English, a concise confirmation meaning “Yes, that's possible.”
“revert by end of day” Reply or come back with an update by EOD.
“go live a little later” Launch later than the other regions without delaying the whole project.
