Day 05 · Numbers, Dates & Names

Understanding isn't enough.
Get the detail right.

Real-world listening often fails on tiny details: 13 or 30, 15 or 50, June 14 or June 40, a person's name, a room number, a time, a price or a reference code. Today you train precision: hear the meaning, then capture the exact data.

Step 2 · Precision Patterns

Train the risky contrasts.

15 min
-teen vs -ty
13 ↔ 30
“ThirTEEN” usually has stronger final stress; “THIRty” stresses the first syllable.
-teen vs -ty
15 ↔ 50
Listen for stress placement, not only the consonants.
Time
2:15 ↔ 2:50
Always verify the minute value in context if it affects a schedule.
Money
$1,850 ↔ $1,815
Large numbers often arrive in chunks. Track thousands, hundreds and tens separately.
TypeListen forStrategy
DatesJune 14Anchor the month first, then the date.
NamesMara / Maya / MariaUse spelling or repetition when the name matters.
CodesB-316Chunk letter + number; do not hold everything at once.
Phone-like strings617-204Write in groups while listening.

Transcript — Client visit update

Hi, quick update for tomorrow. The client is still arriving on June fourteenth, but her flight now lands at nine thirty instead of nine fifteen.

Her name is Mara Chen — Mara with an “a” at the end — and she'll be coming with Daniel Brooks.

I've booked meeting room B-three-sixteen from eleven to twelve thirty. If the flight is delayed, we can move lunch from twelve forty-five to one fifteen.

Finance also confirmed the deposit is one thousand eight hundred and fifty dollars, not one thousand eight hundred and fifteen.

The internal reference is six-one-seven, two-zero-four. I've sent the details to Priya Nair as well, so she should have the same information.

14 vs 40 Stress is a major clue. “fourTEEN” contrasts with “FORty”.
9:15 vs 9:50 Do not rely on context alone. Confirm the final stress and vowel quality.
Mara Chen Names should be treated as data. If uncertain, spelling or repetition is appropriate.
B-316 Chunk letters and numbers separately: “B” + “three-sixteen”.
$1,850 Track the number in groups: one thousand / eight hundred / fifty.
617-204 Short codes are easier if you write them in chunks as they arrive.
